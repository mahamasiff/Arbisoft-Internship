Cat Care Guide:

1. Nutrition & Hydration
- Diet: Cats are obligate carnivores. Their diet must primarily consist of meat-based proteins.
- Water: Cats naturally have a low thirst drive. Provide fresh water daily, preferably using a pet water fountain to encourage drinking. Wet food is highly recommended to supplement water intake.
- Toxic Foods: Never feed a cat chocolate, onions, garlic, grapes, raisins, or macadamia nuts.

2. Litter Box Maintenance
- Rule of Thumb: Have one litter box per cat, plus one extra.
- Cleaning: Scoop solid waste daily. Completely empty, wash with mild soap, and refill the box with fresh litter every 1 to 2 weeks.
- Placement: Keep litter boxes in quiet, accessible, and low-traffic areas away from their food and water bowls.

3. Grooming
- Brushing: Shorthair cats need weekly brushing. Longhair cats (like Persians or Maine Coons) require daily brushing to prevent painful matting.
- Claws: Trim nails every 2-3 weeks to prevent overgrowth and protect furniture. Provide sturdy scratching posts.

4. Environment & Enrichment
- Vertical Space: Cats feel safe when elevated. Provide cat trees, window perches, or accessible shelving.
- Playtime: Dedicate 15-20 minutes daily to interactive play using wand toys or laser pointers to simulate hunting and prevent boredom.
- Hiding Spots: Always ensure your cat has a quiet, enclosed space to retreat to when they feel stressed or overwhelmed.

5. Veterinary Care
- Annual Checkups: Adult cats require at least one veterinary visit per year for wellness exams and vaccinations.
- Senior Cats: Cats over the age of 7-10 should see a vet bi-annually for blood work and age-related monitoring.
